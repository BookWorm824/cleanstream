CleanStream (test version)
==========================

CleanStream mutes bad language while you watch YouTube, Netflix, Prime Video
and Hulu (and Disney+, Max, Peacock and Paramount+). Nothing is copied or
saved: it all happens as you watch.


Putting it in Chrome (one time)
-------------------------------
1. In Chrome, type   chrome://extensions   in the address bar and press Enter.
2. Turn on "Developer mode" (the switch at the top right).
3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose this folder:
       Downloads\CleanStream\extension
4. Click the puzzle piece at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to
   CleanStream, so its green button stays in view.
5. If YouTube was already open, reload that page.

In Microsoft Edge it's the same, but type   edge://extensions   and the
"Developer mode" switch is on the left.

Chrome may say once in a while that a "developer mode extension" is on.
That's normal for a test version; just close the message.


Getting a new version
---------------------
Double-click "Update CleanStream" in this folder. It gets the newest
CleanStream and puts it in the "extension" folder. Within a minute Chrome
picks it up by itself; then press F5 on any YouTube page.
The CleanStream settings window says when a new version is ready.
(The first time, Windows may say it protected your PC: click "More info",
then "Run anyway".)


Using it
--------
- Open any YouTube video. A small line at the top left of the video says
  how many words will be muted (or why it can't).
- Click the green CleanStream button for the settings:
    Filtering for: Young kids / Kids / Teens / Adults
    Mute: the kinds of words to mute (the same groups as CleanView)
    Your own words: anything else you always want muted
  "This video" shows what was found and when.
- The switch at the top turns CleanStream off and on.


Good to know
------------
- It needs the video's captions (the auto-made ones are fine). A video with
  no English captions can't have its language muted.
- If captions are on, bad words in them are shown as d***.
- On Netflix, Prime Video, Hulu and the others CleanStream reads their
  English subtitles (without showing them on Netflix and Prime) and mutes a
  little wider than on YouTube, since they give a line at a time.
- On Disney+, Max, Peacock and Paramount+ (and Hulu, if it says so), turn on
  English subtitles (CC) in the player: CleanStream reads them as they come
  and hides them on screen. If the subtitles still show, bad words in them
  are shown as d***.
